About Us

Community Care, Advocacy & Learning

Black Moms NB is a community-rooted organization supporting Black and African-descended children, youth, families, and communities through connection, advocacy, learning, and collective care.

We create spaces for people to share experiences, access support, strengthen community connections, and navigate systemic barriers with guidance and care.

Featured Initiative

Black Moms NB Education Project

A community-led initiative examining anti-Black racism in New Brunswick's K–12 education system — through research, dialogue, and collective action.

Webinar

Anti-Black Racism in Education: What the Research Tells Us

Saturday, August 8, 2026 — 1:00–2:30 pm

A discussion about what the research tells us about anti-Black racism in the K–12 education system, and how it plays out in New Brunswick.
